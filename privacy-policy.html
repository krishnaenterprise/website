Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Krishna Enterprise & Services (“KES”, “we”, “our”) respects your privacy. This page explains how information may be handled when you visit our website or contact us.

If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, you may share your name, contact details, service enquiry and documents or information needed for that enquiry. Please share only information necessary for the service you are requesting.

How we use information

We use information you provide to respond to enquiries, explain service requirements, coordinate assistance and communicate about your requested work. We do not sell your personal information.

Links on this website may open WhatsApp, Google Maps, Google review pages or other third-party services. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control their practices.

Data safety and retention

We take reasonable care when handling information shared with us. Please avoid sending passwords, OTPs, full payment-card details or other highly confidential information. We retain enquiry information only as reasonably needed for communication, service delivery or applicable legal requirements.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about information you have shared or request correction or deletion, subject to records we must retain by law or for legitimate business purposes.
