Krishna Enterprise & Services ("KES", "we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website or contact us about our services.

Please read this policy carefully. By using our website or choosing to share information with us, you acknowledge the practices described below.

1. Information You May Share With Us

Depending on the service you request, you may choose to provide information such as your name, phone number, email address, messages, and details or documents needed to respond to your enquiry or assist with the requested service. Please share only information that is relevant and necessary.

2. How We Use Information

We use information you provide for purposes such as:

  • Responding to enquiries and communicating with you.
  • Understanding and processing the service you request, including RTO, documentation, property, website-building, and poster or banner services.
  • Providing service updates and customer support.
  • Maintaining the security and proper functioning of our website and business records.
  • Meeting applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only when reasonably necessary to provide a service you have requested (for example, with a relevant service provider or authority), to operate our website or communications, to comply with applicable law, or to protect our rights and prevent misuse. We aim to share only the information needed for that purpose.

4. Documents and Sensitive Information

If a requested service requires identity, vehicle, property, financial, or other supporting documents, please send them only through a channel we have provided for that service. Avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, PINs, or banking credentials. We will not ask you to disclose these security credentials.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information shared with us from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain relevant business records, or to meet legal requirements. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Cookies and Third-Party Services

Our website may be hosted or delivered through third-party platforms, such as GitHub Pages. Those platforms may process technical information, including basic usage or server information, under their own privacy terms. Links to third-party websites or services are governed by the privacy policies of those providers. We do not control their practices.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to ask about the personal information you have shared with us, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask us to delete information where we are able to do so and are not required to retain it by law. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for people seeking business or service-related assistance. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children. A parent or guardian may contact us if they believe a child has shared information with us.

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, website, or legal requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
